### PLG Benchmarks 2025-2026 (ProductLed Survey, 600+ Companies)

**Free Model Conversion Rates:**

| Model | Visitor→Free | Free→Paid (Median) |
|-------|--------------|---------------------|
| Freemium | 12% | 9% |
| Free Trial | 5% | 9% |

**Key insight:** Freemium converts visitors 140% better than free trials, but both convert to paid at the same rate (9%).

**Conversion by ACV:**

| ACV Range | Free→Paid Conversion |
|-----------|---------------------|
| $1K-$5K | **10%** (highest) |
| $5K-$10K | 8% |
| <$1K | 7% |
| >$10K | 6% |

**Product Qualified Leads (PQLs):**
- Companies using PQLs: Only 24%
- PQL→Paid conversion: **25%** (vs 9% without PQLs)
- ACV $5K-$10K with PQLs: **39% conversion**

> PQLs are massively underutilized. 76% of companies don't track them at all.

**What Are PQLs?**
Product signals indicating buying intent:
- Activation points reached
- High-value feature unlocks
- Usage thresholds crossed
- "Aha!" moment experiences

**Team Ownership:**
- PLG Strategy: Product (50%), Marketing (42%)
- Free→Paid Conversion: Sales (23%), Customer Success (15%)
- Upsells: Sales + CS (58%), Product only 10%

### State of PLG 2026 (UserGuiding)

**The Harsh Reality:**
> "For half of all products, more than 98% of new users are inactive two weeks after their first action." — Amplitude 2025 Benchmark

**Three Macro Shifts:**
1. Users try more tools but commit to fewer
2. Sales cycles compressed (even enterprise)
3. AI changing discovery and education

**Product-Led Sales (PLS) Evolution:**
- PLG + Sales = PLS (not either/or)
- Even strongly PLG companies use sales for:
  - High-value accounts
  - Custom configurations
  - Data migrations
  - Multi-user setups
- Sales engage based on product signals, not cold outreach

**AI in PLG:**
- Users asking AI agents instead of Googling
- Need to optimize content for AI consumption AND SEO
- AI-powered in-app assistants becoming standard
- Onboarding material creation automating

**Personalization Evolution:**
- 2020s: "Hi {first_name}"
- 2026: Foundational pillar of entire product experience
- Onboarding surveys collecting:
  - Primary use case
  - Technical expertise level
  - Tools switching from
  - Workflows to accomplish

**Onboarding Shift:**
- Old: Long, rigid, mandatory tours
- New: Short, flexible, optional flows
- Focus: Accelerating time-to-value
- Max 3-4 steps in initial tours
- Contextual help over upfront education
