## SaaS Valuation Multiples 2026 (Windsor Drake)

### Current Market State

| Segment | EV/Revenue Multiple |
|---------|---------------------|
| Public SaaS median | 6-7x |
| Top-quartile public | 13-14x |
| Bottom-quartile public | 1-2x |
| LMM private ($5-50M EV) | 4-5x |
| Bootstrapped SaaS | ~4.8x |
| Equity-backed SaaS | ~5.3x |

### Historical Context
- 2015-2017: 5-6x
- 2021 peak: 18.6x (bubble)
- 2022-2023: 60% correction
- 2025-2026: Stabilized at 6-7x

### Private Market Stratification

| Enterprise Value | Revenue Multiple | EBITDA Multiple |
|------------------|------------------|-----------------|
| $5-10M | 3-4x | 8-11x |
| $10-25M | 4-5x | 10-13x |
| $25-50M | 5-7x | 12-16x |

### What Drives Premium Multiples (7-9x)

**1. Rule of 40 Performance**
- Growth rate + EBITDA margin ≥ 40%
- Scores >50% with NRR >120% = 7x+ revenue
- Now the SINGLE strongest predictor (beats growth alone)

**2. Net Revenue Retention**
| NRR | Typical Multiple |
|-----|------------------|
| <90% | ~1.2x |
| 100-110% | ~6x |
| >120% | 8x+ |

The relationship is nonlinear - small NRR improvements above 110% create disproportionate multiple expansion.

**3. Gross Margin & Revenue Quality**
- Pure SaaS: 75-85% gross margins expected
- Subscription revenue >80% of total = "pure-play" classification
- Below this = multiple discount

**4. Vertical Specialization**
- Vertical SaaS: 25-30% premium over horizontal
- Best performers = "system of record" status
- Embedded fintech can generate 30-40% of revenue at 40-60% margins

### Strategic vs PE Buyers

| Buyer Type | 2025 Share | Premium vs PE |
|------------|------------|---------------|
| Strategic | 62% | 1.5-2.0x |
| Private Equity | 38% | Baseline |

**PE activity:** Q1 2025 = 73 PE-led SaaS transactions (66% increase over 2024)

### The $30M Delta Example
- $10M ARR SaaS at 4x = $40M EV
- Same company at 7x = $70M EV
- **Difference = $30M** just from positioning/metrics

## Top SaaS Ideas 2026 (Hostinger Analysis)

### Four Profitable SaaS Drivers

1. **AI-powered solutions** - 92% of companies adopting in next 3 years
2. **Modular/flexible platforms** - Reduce churn, encourage long-term subs
3. **Customer experience optimization** - CX-focused companies 2x more profitable
4. **Micro SaaS model** - 70-80% profit margins typical

### High-Potential Verticals

**1. Accessibility Compliance Scanner**
- EU European Accessibility Act obligations taking effect 2025
- Automated audits + regression monitoring + proof-of-effort reporting
- Build difficulty: Medium

**2. AI Agent Orchestration**
- Deploy, control, measure agents across workflows
- Workflow agent builder + control room dashboard + outcome tracking
- "The winners won't be another chatbot"

**3. License & Vendor Management**
- Companies stack more SaaS = unused licenses drain budgets
- Smart license tracking + renewal insights
- Unsexy but essential

**4. Ecommerce Infrastructure**
- One-click mobile checkout
- Cross-platform inventory sync
- Automated upsells/subscriptions

### Micro SaaS Pattern
> "A good Micro SaaS idea usually looks like this: one clear outcome, minimal setup, and a workflow people repeat every week."

Examples from the "unsexy needs":
- Compliance checklists
- AI spend alerts
- Accessibility scans
- Simple onboarding portals

## Key Frameworks Extracted

### The Starter Story Compounding Formula
1. Start scrappy (Starbucks + side project)
2. Outreach, edit, publish - repeat daily
3. Don't quit when graphs are flat
4. Recommit when walking away would be easier
5. Double down on what's working, just slower than expected
6. Add video when ready to scale
7. Stay lean (3 FTE max)
8. 8 years → "overnight success"

### Media Acquisition Thesis
HubSpot's framework for acquiring media brands:
- Trusted, creator-led brands > programmatic reach
- Audiences that actively seek out content (not served by retargeted ads)
- "Mindset match" > demographic match
- YouTube + newsletters = future of B2B lead gen

### Exit Multiple Optimization Checklist
To move from 4x to 7x revenue multiple:
- [ ] Rule of 40 score ≥50%
- [ ] NRR ≥110% (ideally 120%+)
- [ ] Subscription revenue ≥80% of total
- [ ] Gross margins 75-85%
- [ ] Clean cohort data (3-5 year)
- [ ] Vertical specialization (25-30% premium)
- [ ] "System of record" positioning if possible
