### 2. SaaS Valuation Multiples 2026 (Windsor Drake)

**Market Context:**
- Public SaaS median: 6-7x EV/Revenue (back to 2015-2016 levels)
- Peak 2021: 18.6x EV/Revenue
- Correction 2022-2023: Compressed 60%+
- 2025 stabilization: Discipline over hypergrowth

**Private Lower Middle Market ($5M-$50M EV):**

| Enterprise Value | EV/Revenue | EBITDA Multiple |
|------------------|------------|-----------------|
| $5M-$10M | 3-4x | 8-11x |
| $10M-$25M | 4-5x | 10-13x |
| $25M-$50M | 5-7x | 12-16x |

**Bootstrapped vs VC-backed:**
- Bootstrapped: ~4.8x median
- VC-backed: ~5.3x median
- Gap driven by growth rates, not quality

**What Drives Premium Multiples (7-9x):**

1. **Rule of 40 Performance** (most important)
   - Growth rate + EBITDA margin ≥ 40%
   - Above 50% with NRR >120% = 7x+ revenue
   - Below 40% = 3-4x revenue (75%+ discount)

2. **Net Revenue Retention**
   - NRR <90%: ~1.2x revenue
   - NRR 100-110%: ~6x revenue
   - NRR >120%: 8x+ revenue
   - Nonlinear relationship above 110%

3. **Gross Margin & Revenue Quality**
   - Pure SaaS target: 75-85% gross margin
   - Subscription >80% of total = "pure-play" classification
   - Services revenue drags down multiples

4. **Vertical Specialization**
   - 25-30% premium over horizontal SaaS
   - "System of record" status = highest premiums
   - Embedded fintech: 30-40% of revenue at 40-60% margins

**Buyer Dynamics:**
- Strategic buyers: 62% of LMM SaaS deals (up from 55%)
- Strategic premium: 1.5-2.0x over PE on comparable deals
- PE record: 73 transactions in Q1 2025 (66% increase YoY)

**The Math:**
> "The difference between a 4x and 7x revenue multiple on a $10M ARR SaaS business is $30 million in enterprise value."

### 3. SaaS Metrics 2026 Benchmarks (Averi.ai)

**Growth Reality Check:**
- $1M-$30M ARR median growth: 26%
- Top performers: 40-50%
- Bootstrapped median: 23%
- VC-backed median: 25%

**Net Revenue Retention:**
- Median NRR: 106%
- Best-in-class: 120-130%
- Enterprise with network effects: 130%+
- Existing customers = 40% of new ARR (50%+ above $50M)

**Gross Revenue Retention:**
- Target: 85-95%
- B2B SaaS minimum: 90%
- Below 80% = "band-aid on gunshot wound"

**Customer Churn:**
- B2B SaaS average: 3.5% annual (2.6% voluntary, 0.8% involuntary)
- "Good" monthly: <1%
- Best-in-class: <3% annually
- Higher ARPU = lower churn

**CAC & Payback:**
- CAC rose 14% while growth slowed
- Early-stage payback: 8-12 months
- Mid-stage ($25M-$50M): 15-18 months
- Large ($50M-$100M): 20-24 months
- ACV >$100K: 24 months median payback

**LTV:CAC Ratio:**
- Minimum viable: 3:1
- Healthy: 4:1 (new standard, was 3:1)
- Excellent: 5:1+
- Too high (7:1+): Under-investing in growth

**Gross Margin:**
- Target: 75%+ for pure SaaS
- Best-in-class: ~90%
- Median: 77%
- Services >20% of revenue = drags below 77%

**ARR Per Employee:**
- Overall median: $129,724
- $1M-$3M ARR: $99,858
- $20M-$50M ARR: ~$175K
- $50M-$100M ARR: $200K
- Target 2026: $150K-$250K
- Bootstrapped > VC-backed at every level

### 4. Micro-SaaS Opportunity Patterns (Elementor)

**Three Build Paths:**

1. **Custom-Coded SaaS** — Complex apps, hire developers, Elementor for marketing site
2. **WordPress-Based WaaS** — "Website as a Service" for niches
3. **Vertical AI Tools** — Niche-specific AI (not generic)

**Hot Categories 2026:**

**AI-Powered:**
- Content repurposing (1 blog → 10 social posts)
- Niche content generators (real estate listings, legal briefs)
- eCommerce support bots (deep integration, not FAQ links)
- Personalization engines (Amazon-level for small stores)
- Vertical image/video analyzers (construction safety, crop health)

**Vertical SaaS:**
- CRM for solopreneurs (simple, not Salesforce)
- Industry-specific project management
- Compliance tools for regulated industries

**WaaS (Website as a Service):**
- "Websites for [Industry]" subscription model
- Template + hosting + support bundle
- Target: Plumbers, restaurants, law firms, real estate

**Key Pattern:** Vertical > Horizontal. Niche dominance > broad competition.
