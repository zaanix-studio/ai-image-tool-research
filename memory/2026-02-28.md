## BVP AI Pricing Framework

### The Three Charge Metrics for AI Products

| Metric | Best For | Trade-off |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| **Consumption-based** (per token/API call) | Technical buyers, developers | Predictable margins but confusing for non-technical users |
| **Workflow-based** (per completed task) | Discrete recognizable tasks | More cost variability but clearer value prop |
| **Outcome-based** (per successful result) | Confident AI performance | Maximum cost risk but perfect value alignment |

**Key insight:** As you move from consumption → workflow → outcome, you accept more cost risk in exchange for tighter customer value alignment.

### 7 Guiding Principles for AI Pricing

1. **Pricing ties to value delivered, not access granted** - Abandon seat-based for usage/output/outcome models
2. **Hybrid and tiered models create predictability with upside** - Base subscription + usage tiers
3. **Pricing must account for inference costs** - AI has real marginal costs (50-60% gross margins vs 80-90% SaaS)
4. **AI tools reimagine traditional budgets** - AI spend sits alongside IT budgets, not replaces
5. **New success metrics redefine "value"** - Time to prototype, % autonomous work, resolution rate
6. **Pricing strategy shapes GTM and customer success** - Pricing is no longer back-office
7. **AI is more like co-workers, not just tools** - Price for outcomes, not access

### AI Pricing Examples

| Company | Model | Pricing | Value Focus |
|---------|-------|---------|-------------|
| Intercom Fin | Outcome | $0.99/resolution | Support efficiency |
| EvenUp | Outcome | Per demand package | Legal time saved |
| DeepL | Hybrid | Per user + per file | Accuracy |
| Leena AI | Outcome | Per ticket closed | HR automation |

**VixPic Application:** BYOK model = hybrid. Base LTD + user pays their own API costs. Perfect alignment since inference cost = $0 for us.

## Golden Door Software Pricing Playbook

### The 4 Ironclad Laws of B2B SaaS Pricing (BVP)

1. **Pricing Is Never Done** - Revisit at least twice/year. WTP is dynamic.
2. **Data-Light ≠ Data-Free** - Even without competitors, comparable products exist for benchmarking
3. **Interdisciplinary Input Is Critical** - Sales, Product, Finance, Marketing, CS all needed
4. **Money Is Only the Tip** - Packaging, positioning, segmentation matter as much as the number

### The 10 Pricing Models

| Model | Typical ACV | Motion | Best For |
|-------|-------------|--------|----------|
| Flat-Rate | $5K-$25K | PLG | SMB, simplicity brands |
| Per-User | $10K-$100K | Sales-Led | Mid-Market |
| Tiered | $5K-$50K | Hybrid | SMB to Enterprise |
| Freemium | $0-$25K | PLG | SMB, Developers |
| Usage-Based | $1K-$500K+ | PLG | All segments |
| Value-Based | $50K-$500K+ | Sales-Led | Enterprise |
| Hybrid | $10K-$200K | Hybrid | Mid-Market to Enterprise |
| Per-Feature | $5K-$50K | Hybrid | SMB to Mid-Market |
| Outcome-Based | $100K+ | Sales-Led | Enterprise |
| AI-Dynamic | Variable | PLG | All segments |

### BVP Pricing Archetypes

Based on ACV size + market maturity:

| Archetype | ACV | Market | Strategy |
|-----------|-----|--------|----------|
| **Mouse** | Low | Emerging | Optimize for volume, self-serve |
| **Gnome** | Low | Mature | Focus on efficiency, PLG |
| **Elephant** | High | Emerging | Invest in sales, education |
| **Godzilla** | High | Mature | Win on differentiation, account expansion |

**VixPic = Gnome** - Low ACV ($29-149 LTD), mature market (AI images exist), so focus on PLG efficiency.
