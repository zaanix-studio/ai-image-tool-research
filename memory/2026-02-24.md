## 🔨 Midnight Build Session (12AM Bangkok / 5PM UTC)

### Built: 4 New Free Tools

**1. Rotate & Flip (`/tools/rotate`)**
- Rotate images by any angle (-180° to +180°)
- Quick presets: -90°, -45°, +45°, +90°, 180°
- Flip horizontal/vertical
- Targets: "rotate image online", "flip image", "mirror photo"

**2. Photo Filters (`/tools/filters`)**
- 12 preset filters: Grayscale, Sepia, Vintage, Warm, Cool, High Contrast, Soft, Vivid, Muted, Dramatic, Bright
- Fine-tune controls: brightness, contrast, saturation, blur
- Real-time preview
- Targets: "photo filters online", "instagram filters free", "sepia filter"

**3. Metadata Viewer (`/tools/metadata`)**
- View image info: dimensions, file size, aspect ratio, megapixels
- Privacy warning about EXIF data
- Strip all metadata with one click (re-encodes to remove EXIF)
- Targets: "exif viewer", "remove exif data", "image metadata"

**4. Image Splitter (`/tools/splitter`)**
- Split images into grid tiles
- Presets: 2×2, 3×1, 1×3, 3×3 (Instagram), 4×4, 2×3
- Custom grid up to 6×6
- Download individual tiles or all at once
- Instagram tip: upload in reverse order
- Targets: "image splitter", "instagram grid maker", "split photo into grid"
