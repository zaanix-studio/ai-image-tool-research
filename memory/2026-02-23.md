## 🔬 Evening Research: AI Micro-SaaS Decision Frameworks (9PM Bangkok / 2PM UTC)

### The Skills-Time-Capital Matrix (New Framework)

Before building ANY AI micro-SaaS, ask three brutally honest questions:

| Question | Pass Criteria |
|----------|---------------|
| Can you build MVP yourself in 2 weeks? | Yes, OR have $2-5K for dev |
| Can you reach 100 customers without paid ads? | Yes (existing audience/community) |
| Is API cost under $0.10 per user action? | Yes (or BYOK model) |

**Critical insight:** Most failed AI SaaS projects die because founders underestimate build time by 300%.

**Example failure:** One founder built a beautiful AI video editor. OpenAI costs were $3/video. He charged $5. Math doesn't work.

> "68% of successful solo founders used AI primarily for development acceleration, not as the core product feature." — Freemius 2025 State of Micro-SaaS

**Translation:** AI should make YOUR life easier, not become your entire moat.

### Competition Saturation Test

Quick filter - search "[your idea] AI tool" on Product Hunt and Twitter:

| Competitors Found | What It Means |
|-------------------|---------------|
| 0-3 | Either goldmine OR nobody wants this (usually latter) |
| 4-12 | **Sweet spot** — validated demand, room to differentiate |
| 15+ | Need killer distribution or hyper-specific niche |

**Red flag:** If top 3 competitors raised funding, they can lose money longer than you can stay solvent. Pick a different battlefield.

### The 7-Day Launch Playbook

**Day 1-2: Build Ugliest MVP That Works**
- No design system. No animations. Just functional.
- Non-technical: Zapier + Make.com + APIs (will be janky, that's fine)
- Technical: Hard-code stuff, use SQLite, resist beautiful architecture

**Day 3-4: Find First 10 Users**
- Post in 3 relevant communities with genuine value
- DM 20 people who complained about the problem
- Offer free access in exchange for feedback

**Day 5-6: Iterate Based on Feedback**
- Fix critical bugs only
- Add ONE feature if repeatedly requested
- Don't add features nobody asked for

**Day 7: Turn On Payments**
- Stripe link. Simple pricing. One tier.
- If someone pays, you have a business
- If nobody pays in week 2, pivot or kill

### Coordination vs Creation (Key Pattern)

> "The best AI micro-SaaS case studies from 2025 all had one thing in common — they solved coordination problems, not creation problems."

**Saturated:** AI writers, AI image generators, AI content tools (creation)
**Underserved:** AI organizers, AI schedulers, AI workflow coordinators (coordination)

**Examples of coordination wins:**
- AI Meeting Scheduler for Podcast Guests (competition 2/10)
- AI GitHub Issue Triage Bot
- AI Curriculum Generator for Course Creators (only ONE competitor)

**VixPic Reality Check:**
- We're in "creation" category (AI image generation)
- BUT: BYOK positioning shifts us to "infrastructure/coordination"
- We organize/coordinate user's AI providers, not just generate
