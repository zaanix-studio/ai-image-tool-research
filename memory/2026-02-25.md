## 2. Build in Public Framework (SignalPath Case Study)

### The Repeatable System

**Core Narrative Formula:**
1. One core promise (every update ties back to specific outcome)
2. One primary audience (specific role, not "everyone")
3. One proof format (demos, metrics, decision logs = verifiable)

### The Ship → Prove → Teach Loop

- **Ship:** Deliver visible improvement weekly with clear user outcome
- **Prove:** Show evidence (speed gains, reduced steps, fewer tickets, conversion lift)
- **Teach:** Explain what you learned so audience benefits even without buying

### Distribution System (Per Ship)

1. One "source of truth" post (blog/changelog) — designed for search
2. 3-5 derivative posts (social threads, short videos) — pointing back to source
3. One customer-facing email — outcome-focused, not release notes
4. One sales enablement snippet — demo timestamp, objection handling

### Three Feedback Layers

1. **Public:** Open posts that surface objections ("Would this break your workflow?")
2. **Insider:** Small group with early access for quick testing
3. **Customer:** In-app prompts tied to events

### Feedback Prioritization Rubric

| Criteria | Question |
|----------|----------|
| Severity | How painful is this today? |
| Frequency | How often does it occur? |
| Reach | How many accounts benefit? |
| Confidence | Evidence or just opinions? |

### Public Roadmap Format (Balancing Openness)

- **Now:** What's actively shipping this week + measurable outcome goal
- **Next:** What's likely coming soon, phrased as user problems
- **Later:** Themes and bets, not commitments

### Founder Post Structure (EEAT-Compliant)

1. Clear claim (e.g., "We reduced setup time")
2. Method (what changed)
3. Evidence (before/after metrics)
4. Limitation (what's still not solved)
5. Next action (what we're testing)

### Metrics That Matter for Build-in-Public

- **Activation:** Time-to-first-success, % completing success event in 24-48h
- **Retention:** Weekly active usage of key workflows (not logins)
- **Support load:** Volume + repeat rate of top 10 tags per release
- **Expansion:** Seats added, usage per account
- **Content-to-revenue:** Self-reported attribution + assisted conversions

### Key Insight
> "Build-in-public works best when you treat content as a system that improves product-market fit, not just a megaphone for announcements."

## 3. SaaS Marketing Case Studies (Multi-Company)

### Case 1: Precision Targeting for Small SaaS
- Problem: Generic social posts, underwhelming returns
- Solution: Buyer intent focus, meaningful segmentation, rapid follow-up
- Results: Lead volume +450%, revenue +72%, conversion 1% → 9%

### Case 2: Paid + Organic Synergy
- Combining paid search, LinkedIn ads, SEO, and ABM simultaneously
- Organic leads +190%
- Demo requests multiplied after landing page revamp
- Key: Channels coordinated around shared goals

### Case 3: SEO-Led Enterprise Growth
- Heavy SEO investment: technical fixes + high-intent keyword content
- Organic traffic 10x in under a year
- Sessions: minimal → thousands
- Key: SEO as strategic engine, not afterthought

### Case 4: Viral/Referral Expansion
- HubSpot: Content + educational resources (blogs, guides, webinars)
- Dropbox: Referral program with storage rewards → customers become growth drivers
- Key: Design incentives that genuinely motivate sharing

### Case 5: Personalization Impact
- Tailored video content → click-through rates well above industry average
- Personalization = customer connection, not just buzzword

### 3 Cross-Cutting Lessons
1. **Know your buyer inside out** — specificity beats generic
2. **Combine channels for momentum** — no silos
3. **Track metrics that matter** — qualified leads, conversion, engagement quality
